Feel.AI Documentation

The AI that speaks like us, feels like us, and connects millions through Pidgin.

Overview

Feel.AI is a Nigerian-built artificial intelligence model designed to understand and communicate in authentic Nigerian Pidgin. It bridges communication gaps, preserves culture, and gives AI a voice that feels local — not foreign.

Current Capabilities

  • Understands and generates natural Nigerian Pidgin text.
  • Handles different regional variations of Pidgin across Nigeria.
  • Processes input, refines grammar and tone, and produces clean, expressive output.
  • Built entirely with Python

Future Vision: Feel.AI Call Mode 🎧

Feel.AI is moving beyond text. The goal is to create a real-time AI that listens, understands, and responds — just like a phone call.

  • Speech-to-text and tone detection for live conversation.
  • Text-to-speech response generation in natural Pidgin voice.
  • Emotion-aware interaction — the AI will sound happy, calm, or serious based on the moment.

Tech Stack

  • Language: Python
  • Frontend (UI Prototype): HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: Flask (for prototype API endpoints)
  • AI Layer: Natural Language Processing (NLP), Speech Recognition, and TTS modules

Data & Training

Feel.AI’s training data is collected from real Pidgin sources — conversations, text samples, and user-generated phrases from different regions (Lagos, Warri, Port Harcourt, Abuja, and more). The model refines this data to capture slang, rhythm, and natural expression without losing context.

Roadmap

  1. Phase 1: Python-based text generation MVP ✅
  2. Phase 2: Integration of speech recognition & voice output 🔄
  3. Phase 3: Real-time conversational AI (Call Mode) 🎯
