About Feel.AI

Feel.AI was born from one simple truth — language no suppose be barrier. Across Nigeria, millions dey express themselves in Pidgin, but technology still dey speak grammar wey no reach street level. That’s where we step in.

We’re building an AI that understands and speaks Nigerian Pidgin naturally — not just translating words, but catching tone, emotion, and street context. Whether you be student, trader, or tech bro, Feel.AI go relate with you the way you talk.

Our mission na to bridge understanding across people and platforms — making communication smoother, faster, and more inclusive for the next generation of digital Africans.

“We feel. We understand. We relate.”

The Future Vision — The Bigger Picture

Feel.AI no just be chatbot — na revolution. We dey build an AI wey talk, feel, and respond like human. From Lagos to Kano, Port Harcourt to Jos — this na movement to make technology truly understand the people. Read the Documentation → to see how the tech works and where we dey go next.

Our dream? To make every Nigerian fit talk to AI like person wey dey hear them well well. Imagine say you fit pick your phone, talk to Feel.AI like normal call — and e dey hear your voice, understand your mood, respond instantly in real Pidgin. That na the future we dey build.

🌍 Local Voices, Global Reach

We start from Naija, but our dream big. Soon, Feel.AI go support more African dialects and connect voices across the continent.

🎙️ Real-Time Voice Interaction

Feel.AI no go just read your words — e go hear you, understand your tone, and talk back instantly like una dey gist for call.

🤖 Emotion + Intonation

We dey train Feel.AI to sense vibe, tone, and emotion — so when you vex, e calm you down; when you happy, e match your energy.

💬 Real Conversations

We no want AI wey dey lecture you — we want one wey dey connect with you. Feel.AI go learn through every chat, every laugh, and every call.
